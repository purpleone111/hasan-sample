01 / the short version

What is
all this?

good question ↓
hello, kind stranger! i am Hasan. a singer-songwriter, composer and very-soon-to-be producer, currently writing sad songs because i never learnt how to be vulnerable normally. i won’t pretend like i know what kind of music it is that I make, but out of the 6000+ genres that the streaming platforms have invented, mine usually comes with the prefix ‘indie’, so folk, pop, alternative, and a mish mash of other genres that i steal from while i write about love (no shit, sherlock), the awkwardness of living and hope in all its forms. when I'm not making music, I'm posing as a multi-disciplinary artist juggling multiple hats on a film set or tanking at a standup comedy gig or having a mind-numbing, debilitating breakdown over all my life decisions. come, explore my little corner on the world wide web instead of doomscrolling on that god forsaken app, take what you like and maybe say hi. I don’t bite . . . unless, you ask . . . i mean i still won’t . . . i have a girlfriend . . . please behave. . . . .
